01

New keys when yours are lost, broken or stuck

Walk away with a working key instead of a problem. We cut and replace keys for homes and businesses, make duplicates so the next lost key is not an emergency, and extract keys that snapped off in the lock.

Covers house keys, padlock and cabinet keys, broken-key extraction and spare sets.

02

Stop worrying about who still has a key

After a move, a roommate change, a tenant turnover or a lost keyring, rekeying resets the lock to a brand-new key — usually cheaper than buying new hardware. Old keys stop working the moment we are done.

Rekeying and master-keying for houses, rentals, offices and matched key sets across several doors.

03

Doors that latch, lock and close properly again

A deadbolt you have to shoulder, a knob that spins, a latch that never quite catches — we repair or replace the hardware and adjust the strike so the door works the way it should.

Lock repair, deadbolt and knob installation, strike-plate and alignment fixes, hardware upgrades.

04

Security for a business that opens and closes daily

Commercial doors take far more use than a front door at home. We work on storefront and office entries, help you control which keys open which doors, and get you back in when a lock fails mid-shift.

Commercial lock service, storefront entry hardware, master-key planning, after-hours call-outs.
